# r3 / Grippable 8 mm common-brick trial

**NOT SLICED. NOT PHYSICALLY TESTED. FULL FIGURE PRINTING IS ON HOLD.**

This is not the old p4 micro-pin/blind-hole test. Start with a few 2x2/2x4 bricks
and a representative 3.2 mm plate. Check handling, stacking and removal first.
The underside is open: stems enter spaces between the perimeter walls and the
outside of the tubes/posts, not every tube bore.

## Confirm the actual equipment

The last reported installed nozzle was 0.2 mm. A 0.4 mm nozzle is also available
and is a comparison candidate for the larger bricks; it is NOT assumed to be
installed. On the slicing computer, select the real P1S/nozzle preset and confirm
PLA brand/color, actual build plate, process preset and layer height.
These files contain no configured manufacturer profile, G-code, print-time or
filament-mass estimate.

## Before printing

1. Open one new trial geometry 3MF and check the quantity and condition labels
   against the catalog. STL is an alternative, not an additional copy. Do not
   start the complete figures' color plates.
2. Keep every bottom at z=0, open underside DOWN and studs UP. Select the actual
   machine/nozzle/material/plate presets. Do not substitute an unrelated printer.
3. Slice locally and inspect the first layer, walls, tubes/posts, roof bridges
   and studs. Stop for filled cavities, missing walls/stems, unstable roof
   bridges or unwanted supports inside the engagement space.
4. Only after reviewing warnings and the actual layer preview, save a separate
   local project. This package performs no Print/Send, login or printer connection.

## Distinguish the two fit variables

The male setting is a STUD DIAMETER CORRECTION. The female setting is RADIAL
clearance between the stem surface and each engaging wall/tube surface.
It is not r2's blind-hole diameter difference. Record the actual labeled pair.
Negative clearance is an intentional nominal interference candidate, not a
freely seatable or validated fit. Do not force it together.
Start with a generous positive clearance and the nominal male diameter.

## Minimum useful observations

- Actual printer/nozzle, PLA, plate, process/layer height and sample IDs.
- Can you grip the bricks and seat them without excessive force? Any play,
  floating gap or bottoming out?
- Does a gently lifted pair retain its own weight? Can it be separated easily?
- After five assembly/removal cycles: looseness, cracks, whitening, damaged
  underside walls/tubes/roof or studs.

Stop for pain, required prying tools, cracking or stress whitening. A successful
small pair still does not validate the complete 18 cm figures' accumulated load,
impact, stability or retention. Physical approval and full printing are separate
gates.
