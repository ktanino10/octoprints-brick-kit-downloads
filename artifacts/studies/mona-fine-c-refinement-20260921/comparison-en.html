What changed

  • Actual initial-C cell locations and color boundaries are replayed at8mmXY/4.8mmZ, prioritizing its forehead/cheek steps, white eye rims, smile, ears and whiskers. This is separate from optimizing original-source IoU.
  • The new partition starts from C's actual parts, with bounded grip/support changes, rather than repainting a fresh large-area sample. Facial colors belong to whole physical parts.
  • 4.8mm is an explicit new half-height family:1.6mm roof and3.2mm open cavity.8mm pitch,4.8mm-diameter/1.8mm-tall studs and the open-underside interface remain fixed.
  • Exposed stud height was about56% of a previous surface course. It is about38% in the refinement, nearer initial C's35%. The comparison therefore checks actual forehead/smile depth, not just color.
  • Actual 12,435 pieces; +1,527 vs previous and -1,402 vs C. More pieces alone do not constitute aesthetic approval.
  • After an actual-native wall probe, a0.20mm upper-perimeter chamfer is applied only to3,763 exterior parts. 8,596 internal half-height parts retain their plain bearing faces. No extra pieces are added; finish variants are real distinct types.

1.6mm is integer assembly bookkeeping, not a thin printable part or an FDM layer height. Actual body-height families are listed per row.

Remaining differences and tradeoffs

  • This does not double C's weak legacy pins. The retained studs are wider than C studs scaled2x;0.2mm XY seams, seated vertical body contact and visible roofs also differ.
  • The360mm registered C body plus6.4mm foundation and top studs gives an actual368.2mm height (+8.0mm vs previous). Equal-screen-height images are not physical-size comparisons.
  • 31 one-cell exceptions have no extended grip; their positions/IDs are disclosed and handling is untested. Independent minimum dimensions may belong to different parts.
  • The2 temporary pedestals are excluded from figure count. Do not remove before actual retention, load and tipping checks.
  • The user's positive direction feedback on the previous pilot does not approve this new candidate or physical printing. No complete plate pack, slicing,G-code or new trial was created.
  • Original-source overlap and C-likeness are different objectives. Any original-source IoU decrease while approaching C is recorded separately, not hidden.
  • The chamfer is not C's open vertical gap and can create a fine bright edge. The true roof above the cavity remains1.6mm; the outer cap above the cavity datum is1.4mm. Some bearing area is reduced. Closed geometry is not a strength or retention approval.
