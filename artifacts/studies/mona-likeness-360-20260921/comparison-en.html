Limits and tradeoffs

  • The 8 mm XY steps, brick seams and 4.8 mm diameter / 1.8 mm tall studs remain; this is not the continuous original surface.
  • A roughly 36 cm figure with 10,908 separately assembled parts is a substantial undertaking. Count alone proves neither likeness, handling nor strength.
  • Two temporary whisker pedestals are required. Do not remove before actual retention checks; whole-figure loads, tipping and friction are untested.
  • Original 3MF print size, initial C target scale and on-screen normalization are distinct. No single-body r2 print data is created.
