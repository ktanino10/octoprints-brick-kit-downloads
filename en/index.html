EARLIER r2 / 2026-09-19 PHYSICAL TRIAL

What the trial revealed:
what needs to change.

“Small and difficult to make”
“Holes are filled with plastic”

This records the earlier 4 mm trial.Finer detail alone did not make assembly easier. Follow-up feedback confirmed that the holes were already blocked before ultrasonic cleaning. The cause has not yet been identified.

Installation of a 0.2 mm nozzle was confirmed beforehand. The actual profile, layer height, material brand, and retention for each condition remain unconfirmed.

DESIRED PRINT OPTIONS / DISCUSSION

A choice of printing methods.

The requested options are “print separately, then assemble” and
“print everything at once.”

Print separate parts, then assemble: This is the redesigned system's scope. Individual parts and color-grouped geometry 3MF layouts are identified as unsliced reference data.
“Print everything at once”: Whether this means a one-piece, assembly-free model or batch-printing separate parts on one plate is still unconfirmed. Color-grouped layouts do not confirm a one-piece preference or approve manufacturing.

This records a future intention. CAD used for the assembly view is not treated as a one-piece printable model or print-ready data. Given the current issues, full-kit printing remains on hold.
