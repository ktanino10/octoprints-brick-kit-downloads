ACTUAL PART COUNTS / CG · ANIMATION · CAD · ASSEMBLY

With more parts,
how do appearance and assembly change?

Compare fifteen cases targeting 1.2×, 1.5×, 2×, 3× and 4× the individual physical part count of each character's 1× baseline. These are not linear scale factors for the model.

New requirement: Mona whiskers without external or temporary assembly supports.

Support-dependent Mona designs are preserved as history and do not fulfill this requirement. A revised geometry, root and assembly sequence is added only when all actual assets and evidence are complete. Hiding supports in the CG does not qualify. Physical retention and slicer-generated print supports remain unvalidated.

The 1× baseline is a comparison assumption, not a user-selected design.

For a consistent comparison, all three baselines use 8 mm common blocks adapted toward initial Fine C's palette and pattern. Mona uses the previous final 12,435 parts. Copilot and Ducky use actual fixed configurations with the same approach, not the earlier r3 counts of 695 and 413.

Targets use round-half-up. Actual counts, actual ratios and differences are shown; tolerance is the larger of 1% of the target or one part. Missed targets and in-progress cases are not counted as complete.

Splitting blocks without changing occupied cells does not improve the silhouette or sampling resolution. Changes to seams, surface partitioning and part distribution are distinguished from changes in overall shape. Counts and render metrics are not aesthetic or manufacturing pass scores.
