Fifteen higher-part designs, organized into three characters. Choose an assembled size and part count, then open the STL package and assembly guide.

Print data is available. Physical printing has not yet been validated.

This is not a ready-to-print list. Every case is unsliced, with physical fit and strength unvalidated. Check a small set of parts first; full-kit printing remains on hold.

1. Choose a model and size

Five variants per character. Images and values belong to the selected actual model.

Checking the published models and downloads.

2. After downloading

  1. Extract the entire ZIP

    STLs are organized by part type; required quantities and colors are in the BOM. The complete package also contains CAD, animation and instructions.

  2. Open parts in your slicer

    Open the STL in a slicer such as Bambu Studio and check the printer, material and nozzle settings. Configured 3MF projects and G-code are not included.

  3. Check a small set of parts

    Check joint fit, retention and assembly aids. The assembled preview is not a one-piece STL: the design uses separately printed parts.

Dimensions describe the assembled figure. Temporary assembly aids are different from slicer-generated print supports. Do not remove an aid before physical retention and stability checks.
