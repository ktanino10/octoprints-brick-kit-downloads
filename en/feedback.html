PHYSICAL FEEDBACK / 2026-09-19

Too small.
Holes filled with plastic.

A record of the first physical trial of the 4 mm joints, published with the contributor's permission. It is retained to inform the next design, not as a success report or manufacturing approval.

They are small and difficult to make. Also, some of the holes are filled with plastic.
This report documents the earlier r2 4 mm trial.
It is not reused as physical evidence for the new 8 mm common blocks. Earlier handling and blocked-hole issues remain distinct; causes, retention and repeated-use durability are unresolved.

What is known / What remains unknown

ItemCurrent record
Trial setSmall 4 mm trial set: 7 types, 11 parts. The hash of the input actually sliced has not been cross-checked.
Equipment and materialBambu Lab P1S; a 0.2 mm nozzle was reported installed before the trial. PLA was assumed. The actual material brand, plate, process profile, layer height, and support settings have not been supplied.
Ease of handlingReported from actual use as “small and difficult to make.” Optimal part dimensions and assembly time remain unknown.
Blocked holesSome holes were reported to be filled with plastic. Blockage was present before ultrasonic cleaning, as confirmed in follow-up feedback. The main cause—geometry, first layers, supports, slicing, or another factor—remains unknown.
Fit and retentionSeating, play, pull-out, and cracks or looseness after five insertion/removal cycles have not been established as numerical or notch-specific results.
Applicability to other revisionsThis record does not demonstrate physical success for the historical 6 mm or the 8 mm designs. It is not a pass for full assembly or strength.

The original geometry remains NOT_SLICED. Individually scraping out small holes or forcing parts together will not be assumed to be acceptable requirements for a finished kit.
