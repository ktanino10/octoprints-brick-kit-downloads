Use initial Fine C as the visual reference.

The published r3 and earlier plate/slope studies were judged insufficiently similar to the original character. That visual assessment is not a failed CAD geometry check, nor is it dismissed as merely viewing an old page.

The new approach resamples the original geometry and primarily uses rectangular blocks and plates. It does not substitute a uniformly enlarged coarse r3 arrangement or specialized curved shells.
