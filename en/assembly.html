Checking the revision and actual manifest.

How to read the data and what remains unvalidated

Match part IDs to the BOM and type-specific CAD, then check color and quantity separately. Rows follow the manifest's step values in ascending order. Parts sharing a step value belong to the same proposed stage.

Positions are in millimeters, using each part's body-bottom center as its origin. Z rotation is about the vertical axis; base Z uses 3.2 mm units. Distinguish 9.6 mm brick bodies from 3.2 mm plates.

Insertion direction −Z is a proposed downward placement from above. It does not prove easy fit, support of upper parts, retention or durability. Consult the revision's original records and 3D view for small-part exceptions, temporary support and special conditions.

Do not begin a full-kit print before physical evaluation, force parts together or assume every small hole must be carved out. Color-grouped 3MF layouts contain separate-part geometry, not a monolithic print or sliced P1S project.
