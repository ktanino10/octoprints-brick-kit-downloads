INITIAL FINE C / PREVIOUS 36 cm / ONE REFINEMENT

Toward initial Fine C,
one step further.

Keeping the approximately 36 cm direction, this comparison targets a forehead, white eye rims and stepped mouth closer to initial Fine C. More parts are acceptable; a low part count is not the goal.

Positive feedback on direction is not final or manufacturing approval.

The previous 36 cm direction received positive feedback, with a request to bring the face and outline closer to initial Fine C. That does not approve the new variant's appearance, fit, retention, whole-model loads or tipping stability.

The 8 mm XY interface and open undersides are retained. Body-height families come from the actual assembly, without assuming the previous 3.2 mm and 9.6 mm values. Colors must belong to actual single-color parts and the BOM, not merely a different rendering texture.

Checking publication status of the Mona refinement comparison.

Awaiting actual refinement images and quantities.

New part counts, types, body heights and completed dimensions await the producer's finalized actual data. Images of the previous 10,908-part model or estimates are not presented as a new refinement.

What changed.

    Remaining differences from initial Fine C.

      Actual part counts and changes from the previous study.

      Initial Fine C's 13,837 parts and the previous 36 cm study's 10,908 parts are separate baselines. A higher part count alone does not validate likeness or ease of assembly.

      Body-height families come from the actual data too.

      Body height, assembly Z spacing and FDM slicing layer height are distinct values. Types count shapes used; parts count placed instances.
